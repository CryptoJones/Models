# Ronin 48 — Acceptable Use Policy

This policy applies to all models in the Ronin 48 suite: SELMA, ABBY, BONES, BRUNO, and ATTICUS.

---

## What These Models Are

The Ronin 48 models are AI decision-support tools — fine-tuned language models designed to assist trained professionals by surfacing relevant statutes, protocols, methodologies, and tactical considerations. They are reference tools, not decision-makers.

Every output is a starting point for a trained professional's judgment, not a replacement for it.

---

## Who Should Use These Models

These models are designed for credentialed professionals operating within their scope of practice or legal authority:

| Model | Intended Users |
|-------|---------------|
| SELMA | Sworn law enforcement officers, detectives, special agents, prosecutors |
| ABBY | Credentialed forensic examiners, digital investigators, crime scene analysts |
| BONES | Certified EMS personnel (EMR, EMT, AEMT, Paramedic) operating under medical direction |
| BRUNO | Trained firefighters, company officers, and incident commanders |
| ATTICUS | Licensed attorneys and law students under attorney supervision |

**These models are not designed for use by members of the general public** in place of professional legal, medical, or emergency services advice.

---

## Who Should NOT Use These Models

- Members of the public seeking legal or medical advice
- Any person operating outside their credentialed scope of practice
- Any person using outputs as a substitute for required professional consultation
- Any person using outputs to make final decisions without professional review

---

## What These Models Cannot Do

- Replace the judgment of a trained professional
- Access real-time data, current events, or live databases
- Account for jurisdiction-specific local ordinances, agency SOPs, or regional protocols without being told
- Guarantee accuracy — all outputs must be independently verified
- Serve as expert witness testimony
- Constitute legal, medical, or tactical advice

---

Use of these models does not create any professional-client relationship. Ronin 48, LLC is not liable for decisions made based on model outputs. Users assume full responsibility for verifying outputs and for any actions taken.

These models are not approved or certified by any regulatory body (FDA, ABA, state medical boards, NFPA, etc.).

---

## Required Disclosures for Agency Deployment

Before deploying any Ronin 48 model in an operational context, agencies should:

1. Have outputs reviewed by a qualified subject matter expert (attorney, physician advisor, training officer)
2. Establish a written policy governing when and how model outputs may be used
3. Train users on the limitations of AI decision support tools
4. Establish a feedback and incident reporting mechanism
5. Conduct periodic accuracy audits against current standards

---

## Reporting Bad Outputs

If a model produces an output that is factually wrong, dangerous, or potentially harmful, report it:

- **GitHub Issues:** [CryptoJones/Models](https://github.com/CryptoJones/Models/issues)
- **Codeberg Issues:** [Ronin48/Models](https://codeberg.org/Ronin48/Models/issues)

Include: the input you provided, the output you received, and what the correct answer should have been.
